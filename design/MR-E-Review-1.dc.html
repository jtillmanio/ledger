Mediums-Review · Part E

Review of what was drawn after the feel pass

What it solves. Since 6 October, features were drawn straight into mockups.html by the engineer who built them, following the feel pass by reading it. Most are built and in use. This part checks each against the feel pass, redraws what drifts, adds the 834px frames that were never drawn, and answers roadmap 6.21: how the universe, author, series and subseries read in Now.

The short answer. The work holds up well. The colour meanings, the slot order, the grey-for-no-meaning rule and the sheet behaviour are kept almost everywhere. The drift is small and specific: one red button that destroys nothing, one toast that grew into a panel, a third chip row at 360px, the stepper’s surface, and the U+26B2 glyph still drawn in phone bars.

E.2 · Roadmap 6.21 · Where the book sits

Universe, series, subseries and author in Now

The rows built in 2.2g name each level by its name alone, so Cosmere, Mistborn and Era Two read as three unrelated lines, and a long universe wraps into the meter. Redrawn as the books ladder: the kind in the telescope ladder’s mono key, each level’s key indented 10px under its parent’s with a hairline elbow, the meter and read / total in fixed columns so they align down the stack.

  • Order is outermost first: universe, else the author when you follow them as Reading; then series; then subseries. At most three rows; a level the book lacks draws nothing, and a book on none (Piranesi) draws no block at all, not an empty one.
  • The key column is 72px, enough for SUBSERIES at 11px mono. Names wrap inside their column (The Realm of the Elderlings takes two lines at 360px) and never push the meter. Universe, series and author are links, underlined; a subseries has no page and stays text.
  • On the tablet and desktop the block sits in the hero’s text column under the author and page count, capped at --lens-max 480px, so the meters line up with the lens on comics.
  • Each meter is a --sunk track with the --faint-deco ring and a --ready fill (D-26), the 12px rollup type (D-119), never the hero’s.
