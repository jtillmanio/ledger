A.3 · Tablet and desktop

All five where there is room

The tablet rail and the 1280px sidebar list all five media by name. The 1024px icon rail has no names to spare, so it uses the same switch-button idea as the phone, as a flyout.

  • Tablet rail (72px). Under the L, a --sunk well holds the five media, each 58 × 46 with an 18px icon over its 11px name; the current one is the raised --surface pill, as the feel pass’s segmented switch draws it. Then the medium’s items (56px targets, unchanged), a rule, Inbox, Progress, Activity, and Search and Settings at the foot. Books is the tallest case: 32 + 250 + 9 × 52 + 3 × 52 + 104 ≈ 1010px, inside an 1112px tablet. Below about 1010px tall the middle of the rail scrolls and the foot stays.
  • Sidebar (224px). Search (Ctrl K) sits under the wordmark, as the built mockups have it. Media lists all five; the current one is open with its items nested under it (the current item keeps the --raise fill and the 3px accent bar), and the other four are one-line links that open their dashboards. Across media holds Inbox, Progress and Activity. The three-way segmented well (§14.6) and the every-medium-expanded list drawn later in capture, settings and bdash both go (MR-D4): five labels don’t fit the well, and five expanded media are 34 rows.
  • Icon rail (64px). One 48 × 44 button shows the current medium’s icon and a chevron; it opens a menu of five (role="menu", arrows move, Esc closes, focus returns). Search is an icon above it. Everything else is the feel pass’s rail, with Progress and Activity added after Inbox.
A.4 · Accents

Music and Games beside the existing three

--m-music is the fuchsia of --t2 and --m-games the blue of --t1, as --m-comics, --m-books and --m-watch already reuse --t5, --t6 and --t7. Both were measured against ready, blocked and danger in both themes (§17.2): the lowest is 34.0 (music vs danger, light) and 30.8 (dark), far over the floor of 15.
