Does the Now hero go horizontal, or larger?Horizontal, as on the tablet, and one step larger at 1280: the cover goes to 168 × 252 (150 × 225 from 1024 to 1279). The number stays 56/60. A bigger number would compete with the 40/44 title and push the lens below the cover. The hero is the main column's width and never wider: 592 at 1024, 656 at 1280 and above, because the column stops growing. The lens is comfortable up to about 480px, where each of its 24 cells is about 18px wide, close to the telescope's 20px rail. Past that the cells read as bars instead of issues, so the lens has a 480px max-width. Here it is 378 and 412.
How many Up next covers? Scroll, or wrap?At 1024 you see four full covers and 80px of the fifth. At 1280 you see five and a 16px sliver of the sixth. Either way it is still one row that scrolls. Wrapping twelve covers would make slot 2 heavier than slot 1. The shelf no longer bleeds to the frame edge, because it would run under the side rail (FP-D18). On a fine pointer, ‹ › buttons page it by the number of visible covers.
Where does Also reading sit?It stays in the hero, in the right half of the footer strip next to Since Tuesday, as on the tablet. It is part of Now (slot 1). Moving it to the side rail would put slot-1 content after slot 4.
Is the completion art band still full-bleed?No, it sits inside the column (FP-D17). On desktop the run page is a centred 720px column. Across 1056px or more, a full-bleed band would be a wall of tone with a small cover in the middle, and the sidebar would cut off one side of it. Inside the column, with --r-xl corners, it lines up with the record card and the thumb bar below it.
