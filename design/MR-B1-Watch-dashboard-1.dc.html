Watch: finish what the feel pass started

What it solves. Feel Review §5 drew the phone dashboard and left three conflicts with rules that stand: its Now counted episodes, its shelf had a Reorder for a list with no stored order, and its Position rails read show → season instead of franchise → series. Every other watch screen (tablet and desktop dashboards, status, the list and its saved views, the sheets, add, franchises, shelves, people, refresh, parked) was never drawn in the feel-pass language. This part resolves the three and draws the rest.

B.1 · The three conflicts

Now counts seasons; Up next has no Reorder; Position reads franchise → series

B1 is a show in no franchise, so Position draws one rail of seasons. B2 is Andor, whose Star Wars franchise adds the coarse rail in its hue with the bracket down to the show’s seasons. Both carry the unit badge seasons.

  • 1 · Season as the unit. The per-episode strip and S4 E3 go. The hero number is the season, S4, in --fs-hero-num mono; the count reads 3 of 5 seasons watched; the thumb bar verb is Mark S4 watched. There is no lens: a show has two to ten seasons, which Position already draws whole, so a This stretch lens would repeat the rail (MR-D8).
  • 2 · No Reorder. Up next is sorted oldest added first and says so. The slot’s control is the All · Film · TV segmented control, which is what you actually change when picking for tonight. A stored queue is MR-Q6; the recommendation is not to add one.
  • 3 · Franchise → series. The coarse rail is the franchise’s titles in release order, each cell sized by its seasons (a film is one), drawn with the franchise’s identity hue as the card’s 3px stripe and the swatch beside Franchise · release order. The fine rail is the show’s seasons. The ladder names both. With no franchise the coarse rail, its bracket and its ladder row are dropped and the ladder says In no franchise: the 1-rail variant (MR-D10, §2.7).
  • Situation stays Paused from the feel pass (FP-D12): shows untouched for four weeks, grey, each with its season verb. Rollup is 24 films · 9 seasons · 4 shows finished; the grey Review 2 parked → line appears only while the parked review is due (part F). TMDB and JustWatch attribution sits at the foot.
