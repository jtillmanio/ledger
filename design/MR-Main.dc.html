Ledger · design review 2 · Mediums-Review · 24 Nov 2026

Five media, one language.

The second design review. It keeps the feel pass’s language (palette 1c, Plex and Newsreader, the five slots, the four colour meanings) and stretches it to five media: navigation that fits Music and Games, Watch finished and its three conflicts resolved, Music and Games designed from nothing, a review of everything drawn since 6 October, and the features nobody has drawn yet.

SummaryDepartures MR-DDecisions MR-QTokens and §17Hand-off

Parts A–F are their own artboards on this page, to the right: A navigation, B watch (two boards), C music, D games, E review, F unbuilt. Every frame is drawn twice, dark then light, on palette 1c; phones are 390 × 844 (360 where width is tight), tablets 834 × 1112, desktops 1280 × 900 and 1024 × 768. Phones show their top; a frame marked scrolled shows the lower slots. Covers are striped placeholders for real art; every figure is sample data (D-7), consistent between frames.

Summary

What this pass lands

  • A · Navigation. The phone bar keeps one medium button and a sheet of five; the tablet rail and the 1280px sidebar show all five by name; the 1024px icon rail uses a flyout. / stays “last used”. Inbox, Progress and Activity sit after a divider at the end of the tab row and in an Across media group. Music takes --t2 fuchsia, Games --t1 blue, both ≥ 30 ΔE from ready, blocked and danger.
  • B · Watch. Now counts seasons (Mark S4 watched); Up next has no Reorder and filters by Film/TV; Position is franchise → seasons with a 1-rail variant. Tablet, desktop, status (with empties), the list and saved views, film, show and season sheets, add with Watched through, the grey-× error, franchises, shelves, people, new-season checks, Where to watch and Park are drawn.
  • C · Music. Album as the unit, square covers, a track lens, discography → album rails, Artists to get into, status, artist pages with release-type toggles and a favourites sort, the album sheet, the song ranker and its list, add with disambiguation, and the Spotify review that never guesses.
  • D · Games. Play through vs in rotation as your call, a stored queue with a through-games total, the time-to-beat bar against your chosen estimate, logged hours, coming out at IGDB’s precision, the add flow (platform, play style, destination), and Your platforms.
  • E · Review. The post-feel-pass work holds up; six things drift and are redrawn (a red Remove, the finish toast, the third chip row, the stepper surface, the 6.21 levels, the bar glyph), and six missing 834 frames are drawn.
  • F · Unbuilt. Parked for every medium, comics’ Coming out and two editors, Activity, offline write states, and search on the phone.

Decisions for you

MR-Q1 … MR-Q24

Each is drawn as recommended; the alternatives are what was considered. MR-Q1 is the navigation question.

#QuestionRecommendationConsidered
MR-Q1How do five media fit the phone bar?REC A medium switcher: Books ▾ in the bar, a sheet with all five (A5, A6).Five text tabs with no wordmark (A2): no room for search or a sixth. Icons with the current one named (A3): four of five are icons alone and the bar reflows. Three + More (A4): two media hidden for good. A bottom nav: the thumb bar owns the bottom. Swiping between media: undiscoverable and fights the shelves’ sideways scroll.
MR-Q2What does / open?REC The medium you used last (unchanged), plus a Start on setting.Always one medium. A cross-media Today page: a sixth dashboard that breaks the five slots and repeats Progress.
MR-Q3Where do Inbox, Progress and Activity live?REC The tab row’s end after a divider; the sidebar’s Across media group; the rails after a rule.Inside the Switch medium sheet only (hides Inbox’s count); a sixth pseudo-medium called Ledger in the switcher.
MR-Q4Where is search on the phone?REC In the bar, every screen; retire the thumb bar’s slot.Both (two buttons for one thing); the thumb bar only (screens without a thumb bar lose search).
MR-Q5Which accents for music and games?REC --t2 fuchsia and --t1 blue. Games vs --brand is 13.4 in light, and is always shown beside its name.Indigo #4143c7 / #8696f5: 18.2 from brand, but 9.8 from comics. Plum #7b38a3 / #c88ef1: 6.3 from comics in dark. Teal: 13.2 from ready.
MR-Q6Does watch get a stored queue?REC No: oldest added first, filtered by Film/TV and genre.A watch queue with Reorder, like comics’; a single Tonight pin.
MR-Q7What fills watch’s Situation slot?REC Paused, as the feel pass drew it (FP-D12).The mockup’s What’s reachable counts (seasons ready, shows never started, films waiting).
MR-Q8Where does a caught-up show go when a new season appears?REC Back to In flight under Watching, with a toast naming the season.Up next (as a new title would).
MR-Q9What does marking an album listened do to its tracks?REC Marks every unmarked track listened; skipped tracks stay skipped.Album status independent of tracks; require every track first.
MR-Q10What is the control on music’s Up next?REC All · New (released in the last 90 days).An artist filter; a stored queue with Reorder.
MR-Q11Do artists get a hue on the discography rail?REC No: grey, since one artist’s rail has nothing to tell apart.A --t* per artist, as franchises have.
MR-Q12What is the song ranking’s scope?REC One list across every artist, viewable by artist or year.One ranking per year (books’ Top); one per artist.
MR-Q13Where do a game’s hours come from?REC You log them (Log time, a stepper).Percent complete; a Steam or PSN sync (accounts, an outside call, and partial coverage); no hours, Position as estimate only.
MR-Q14Which time-to-beat estimate measures a game?REC Your choice per game, main by default.One global setting.
MR-Q15Where do set-aside rotation games go?REC A collapsed Set aside group at the foot of In flight.Done’s Stopped (but they never finish); not listed anywhere.
MR-Q16How does the parked review read with several media?REC One review, a group per medium.One list by age, mixed media; a separate review per medium.
MR-Q17One parked cadence or one per medium?REC One.Per medium (five settings for one habit).
MR-Q18Steppers on --sunk or --raise?REC --sunk, as §9 says, sheets included.--raise, as the batch and finish sheets are drawn.
MR-Q19Cap the finish toast?REC Yes: one chip and +N · Follow….Keep it as built; move the offer to a sheet that opens after the finish.
MR-Q20Where do undated reads go in Activity?REC One No date group at the very end.A No date group in every year.
MR-Q21When does the Metron check for comics releases run?REC Only when you tap Check now.A weekly background job (still never at render).
MR-Q22How does Where to watch refresh?REC Only on Check now; greyed after 30 days.On opening the sheet: an outside call when a page renders, which the rules forbid.
MR-Q23Game cover shape?REC 2:3, IGDB art cropped a little.A 3:4 box and new cover tokens.
MR-Q24Rule check: is Log time a “mark verb”?REC Yes: the thumb bar’s first slot is the medium’s commonest write. For games that is logging time, not finishing.Make ✓ Finished primary and Log time secondary, which keeps the rule literal and buries the daily action.
