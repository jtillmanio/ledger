LEDGER · STEP 3 · STYLE GUIDE AND DATA PRESENTATION · 2026-09-30

Horizontal telescope and a calmer, denser system

Every frame here renders the proposed tokens from tokens.css. The telescope is drawn by the real allocate() floor-then-distribute rule, so its geometry is computed, not placed by hand. The written spec is in STYLE_GUIDE.md: geometry sheet, state matrix, type table, contrast and ΔE tables, changelog, Tailwind appendix, proposed deviations and the §1 checklist.

Decision 1
Horizontal on every width
On a phone, a vertical rail's slider drag is the same gesture as page scroll. Horizontal rails scrub with the thumb, take about 150px of height instead of about 354px, and put Also in flight above the fold. Proposed deviation D1.
Decision 2
Same allocate(), new axis
H becomes W. min 14 on phone, 16 on desktop; gap 2; head ±2 kept; at most two stubs. Rail keys sit in a fixed left gutter (44 / 72px), so no label can collide with a bracket.
Decision 3
Dark is the hero
A near-black page, tonal elevation and 1px inner highlights. --sunk now sits below --surface, so tracks and empty cells read as recessed. The only glow is the halo on the head, which marks position.
Unchanged
Four colours, same copy
Green is read, blue is position, amber is held, red is destructive only. No token or class is renamed. Sample figures are illustrative (D-7).
