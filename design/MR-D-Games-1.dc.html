Mediums-Review · Part D

Games, designed from nothing

What it solves. Games is a planning tool for what to play next. A game is one of two kinds, and the kind is your call, not IGDB’s genre: a game you play through goes want → playing → finished or dropped, like a book; a game you keep in rotation (Civilization, Slay the Spire) goes want → in rotation → set aside and never reaches Done. The queue is stored, in your order, as comics’ is.

Decisions it rests on. Position is the time to beat: a bar scaled to the completionist estimate, with the hours you have logged filled --ready and measured against the estimate you chose for that game (main by default). Hours are logged by you (Log time, MR-Q13); nothing is read from a store. Game covers use the 2:3 boxes the other media use (IGDB’s 3:4 art is cropped a little).

Sample data. Expedition 33 on PS5 is in hand: 21 h logged, main story about 30 h. Three games are in rotation. The queue is 10 games, 9 to play through for about 140 h. Release dates are illustrative.
