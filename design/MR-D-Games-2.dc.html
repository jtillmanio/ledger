D.2 · /games/status
  • The total reads 9 games to play through · about 140 h, summing each game’s chosen estimate. A game in rotation adds nothing and says no total; a game IGDB has no estimate for says no estimate, in --faint. Neither is guessed.
  • Grouping (None · Platform · Genre · Play style) is a segmented control; each group head carries its count and its hours. The queue order holds inside a group, and reordering happens from None.
  • Rotation games set aside collapse under Set aside at the foot of In flight, not under Done’s Stopped, because they never finish and may come back (MR-Q15). Dropped play-through games go under Done’s Stopped with a grey dropped badge, as books’ DNF.
D.3 · The game sheet, add, coming out, settings
  • The sheet’s status control changes with the play style: Want · Playing · Finished · Dropped · Park to play through, Want · In rotation · Set aside · Park in rotation. Time to beat lists IGDB’s three estimates and you choose which one measures you (MR-Q14). Release dates are listed per platform.
  • Adding asks three things in one sheet: which of your platforms (one the game isn’t on is aria-disabled with the reason under it, never hidden), Play through · In rotation with a suggestion in grey, and Up next · Someday. An unreleased game skips the last question and goes to Coming out.
  • Coming out: Out now (the last 30 days) with an Up next button, then groups at IGDB’s precision (a day, a quarter, a year, TBA). Refresh dates is a check you start. A moved date keeps its old one under it in --faint.
  • Your platforms are Own · Will own · No. Game shelves are 5.1’s shelves for games, drawn as watch’s (B30).
