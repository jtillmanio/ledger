Mediums-Review · C · Music · continued
C.3 · /music/artist/[id] and the album sheet
  • The artist’s count and meter measure only the release types switched on: Studio albums by default; EPs · Singles · Live · Compilations are chips you add. The follow control is Listening · Following · Not following, the books follow’s three levels in music’s words.
  • Each album: status as glyph and text (✓ listened, ◐ 6 / 11, to listen), your rating in mono, and a ⋯ with Not for me, which hides it and takes it out of the count; hidden albums collapse under Not for me · 1 at the foot.
  • Favourites sorts by the song ranking: median rank first, then songs in your top 10, then how many are ranked, shown on each row as median rank 12 · 3 in your top 10 · 4 of 11 ranked. An album with nothing ranked sorts last.
  • The album sheet: tracks in order with lengths in mono, each a 44px tick (listened, skipped dashed, the next one ringed), the start date, the rating, the relisten history with Log a relisten, and Rank these songs.
C.4 · The song ranker
  • Built as books’ thisorthat is: two cards, each one button with the cover, a kicker (Just listened for the song being placed, its rank for the other), the song and its album; the mono Pick 3 of at most 8 (a binary search over the ranked list); grey Skip. Picks write nothing until the song lands; Esc leaves it unplaced.
  • Resuming keeps every song already placed, listed with its rank, and offers the next one. The ranked list is one list across artists (MR-Q12) with chips to view an artist or a year. A drag handle shows for a fine pointer; every row’s ⋯ opens Move to…, a numeric field with Top · Up one · Down one, for touch and keyboard.
C.5 · Add and Spotify
  • Search shows each artist with MusicBrainz’s note (UK rock band), so same-named artists are told apart by you, not guessed. Adding follows the artist and lists its studio albums (others behind chips) with the likely ones ticked; Add 7 albums to Up next names the count. Skip follows without queueing anything.
  • Spotify follows that don’t resolve to exactly one MusicBrainz artist wait under Needs you on the Artists page: Choose between possible matches, or Search when there are none. Nothing is guessed, and nothing unmatched is followed. Albums the sync queued carry Added from your Spotify follows.
  • Parked albums take Park on the album sheet’s status control, and come back in the review (part F).
