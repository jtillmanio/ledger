Mediums-Review · Part C

Music, designed from nothing

What it solves. Music is shaped like watch: artist → album → track, as franchise → title → season. Nothing plays here; the Ledger records what you have listened to and helps you pick the next album. It starts empty, its catalogue is MusicBrainz with covers from the Cover Art Archive, and its one sync is Spotify saying which artists you follow.

Decisions it rests on. The album is the unit you mark (Mark Sempiternal listened); tracks are optional detail inside it (MR-Q9). Album covers are square, drawn at the same width tokens as posters and book covers (MR-D12). Position is a two-rail telescope, the artist’s discography over the album’s tracks, with the same bracket and ladder as comics and watch.

Sample data. Sempiternal (Bring Me the Horizon, 2013) is in hand everywhere: 6 of 11 tracks listened, track 5 skipped, track 8 next. Two of seven studio albums are listened. Up next holds 24 albums from 38 followed artists.

C.1 · /music

The five slots, filled for albums

  • Now: the square cover, Up now, the album in Newsreader, the artist as a link, its length in mono, then This album: one cell per track, the comics lens’s vocabulary (FP-D3). Listened is --ready, the next track has the --brand ring and halo, a skipped track is the dashed inset (the read-elsewhere mark: grey, never red). Also holds the second album in flight with a 32px verb.
  • Up next: albums from artists you follow, oldest added first, with All · New as its control (New: released in the last 90 days, MR-Q10). An album the sync added says Added from your Spotify follows; an announced one is a grey Coming Mar 14 on a neutral placeholder.
  • Position: Discography · release order over Album. Cells on the coarse rail are sized by track count, measured against the release types you chose for that artist (studio albums by default), never the whole MusicBrainz catalogue. Situation: Artists to get into, followed artists with nothing listened, each with its first album’s verb. Rollup: 31 albums · 9 new artists · 6 relistens.
  • Thumb bar: Mark Sempiternal listened · + Add. Marking the album listened marks its unmarked tracks listened and leaves a skipped one skipped (MR-Q9).
