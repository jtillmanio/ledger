B.6 · Add

Films and shows in one search, Watched through, and the error

  • Search starts after three characters and a pause; the count is announced politely. A title you already have shows its state (✓ watched) in place of Add. A show carries Watched through as a segmented control, none · S1 · S2 · S3; adding Severance at S2 marks seasons 1 and 2 watched with no date.
  • The error is the neutral line with a grey × (§9), never ⚠ and never red: nothing destructive happened. Watch has no add-by-hand path (D-28), so the one primary is Save to inbox.
B.7 · Franchise, shelves, people
  • The Release · Chronological control sits above the tree. Chronological adds Unplaced in chronology at the foot for titles with no chronological place (an anthology, a show that spans eras), each with Place it. The franchise’s meter fills --ready against the titles you listed in it, never TMDB’s catalogue.
  • A shelf holds films and shows together in your order: the handle for a fine pointer and the row’s ⋯ (Move to top · Move up · Move down · Remove from shelf, not red) for touch and keyboard, as books’ shelves (5.1b). Each title’s note sits under it.
  • A person page lists only what is in your watchlist, grouped by role, each group’s watched / listed in mono. Find their other work is a search you start in Add; the page itself calls nothing.
  • Parked is on the film and show sheets’ status control. A parked title is on no tab and not under Paused; it comes back in the review (part F, F1).
