F.1 · Parked, for every medium

Not now, but not gone

One review, one cadence, a group per medium in the nav’s order, longest parked first inside each. The verbs are the same three positions everywhere (keep, bring back, let go), worded for each medium.

  • Grouped, not one list: the verbs differ by medium, and a group lets you clear one medium in a visit. A medium with nothing parked draws no group. Done reviewing records the review for all of them (MR-Q16, MR-Q17).
  • Every verb is grey (.btn.sec), one write with an Undo toast; Keep parked folds the row to Kept parked · Undo. Phone rows wrap their verbs under the text at 44px; from the tablet up they sit at the row’s end at 32px.
  • The dashboards’ one line (Review 2 parked →) appears on every medium’s rollup while the review is due, and opens /parked scrolled to that medium.

F.2 · Comics

Coming out, prerequisites, the volume map

  • Coming out lists the next issues of runs you follow, out now first, then by week and month, dates in mono. Check now asks Metron; nothing is checked on its own (MR-Q21). Found issues are reviewed in a sheet before anything is added, each a checkbox; an annual is unticked with the reason, because it may belong elsewhere in the order.
  • The loop error is a validation failure, so it is the one red on this page, in --danger-soft with a --danger ring and an × (never ⚠). It names every run in the loop in reading order, and Save is aria-disabled with the reason, not hidden.
  • The volume map asks one question at a time: which series a bare “Captain America #1” means, in this run or everywhere, with the candidates’ spans and sizes. Its count of rules to review stays in Settings → Data.
