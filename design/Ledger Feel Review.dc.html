1 · Diagnosis, ranked by how much each one hurts

Why it feels flat

1
The art is missingHURTS MOST

Comics and books are visual things, and every cover in mockups.html is a 36 × 54 tile of initials (“NM”, “THR”). The thing that makes a run recognisable at a glance is absent, so every screen is text on grey and every run looks like every other. The style guide's October note says spine rows already have real covers, but the dashboards don't use them. Fixed by P1.

2
The mark gets no answerHIGH

Mark #141 read is the action you repeat most, and the only response specced is a 300 ms segment growth on a run rail where one issue is about 0.7 px wide, usually below the fold. The button doesn't change, no count moves, and nothing tells you you're now at 46. The finish moment is a toast. Fixed by P2.

3
Nothing is the heroHIGH

The comics dashboard is eight groups with the same ring, radius and 48 px rows. The telecard competes with “Rest of block 1”, and “The frontier” is four equal stat tiles. With no single focal point the page reads as a report, not a place you arrive at. The five-slot order is right; the slots just all carry the same visual weight. Fixed by P3.

4
Colour only shows at badge sizeMEDIUM

The four-meaning rule is not the cause, and I'd keep it. The trouble is where those meanings appear: on 11 px badges, at low chroma, against a cold blue-grey. In dark mode the page (luminance .0036) and surface (.0074) are almost the same, so cards barely separate and the screen reads as dim rather than designed. Green and blue need to fill larger areas wherever they are true, at higher chroma, on a ground with more character. Fixed by P4.

5
Finished things disappearMEDIUM

The library exists only as numbers, and the one library-wide figure is deliberately played down (“73 of 10,834 issues logged… not progress”). That's right for a progress bar, but it leaves nothing in its place. What you've read leaves the dashboard and nothing builds up where you can see it, so you never feel pride in the collection. Fixed by P6.

6
It talks like a databaseLOWER · CHEAP

“costs 4,173”, “The frontier”, “PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF”, “Kept small and last: it is provenance…”. The type has no voice either: one sans at 600 for every title and mono for every number, so names and moments look the same as metadata. Fixed by P5 and P7.

2 · Colour options: the comics dashboard in each

More pop, same four meanings

All three keep green for read, blue for here and primary, amber for held, and red only for destructive actions, which never appear on a dashboard. Each raises chroma by about .03 to .05, separates dark surfaces by three to four L steps instead of one, and lets the content add colour: cover art, and an ambient tone taken from the cover (see departure D6). They differ in the ground. A is cool like your CFB project, B is warm paper and lamplight, C is a tinted night. I recommend B: it adds the warmth you asked for without competing with the blue that marks your position.

How to choose · measured 5 Oct

3 · The mark-read moment

Tap, answer, move on

This all happens in about 1.3 seconds, and it is calm: nothing bounces, loops or celebrates. The motion confirms the mark; the reward is seeing the stack and the counts grow. Every step works under prefers-reduced-motion, where all durations go to 0 and you get the same states without movement.

0 msPress. The button scales to .97 and darkens (--dur-1). The write is optimistic. 120 msConfirm. The button turns --ready and reads “✓ #141 read”. Cell 42 in “This stretch” fills (--dur-4, a 12% settle). A green “✓ read” stamp lands on the cover. The counts tick: 45 → 46 read, 14 → 15 since Tuesday, 212 → 213 this year. The run rail segment grows and the head pill slides to 43. The toast enters: “#141 read · 46 of 381 in Uncanny X-Men · Undo”. +600 ms holdThe green holds for --dur-hold so the confirmation actually registers. 1.3 sNext. #142 slides in from the right. It's dashed and grey, read in another run, so the hint is grey, never green. The button returns to blue as Mark #142 read. #141 joins the “Since Tuesday” stack. 8 sThe toast's 2 px countdown runs out and the toast leaves. Undo reverses every step.
4 · /books, reading and between books (the empty state)

A book in hand, or a pause that feels fine

Same five slots as comics. Position is measured against the quarter, never the backlog. When nothing is in progress, the Now slot remembers what you just finished and offers the top of the plan, without nagging.

5 · /watch

A still, a season, a shelf of posters

Now leads with a 16:9 still and a one-cell-per-episode strip for the current season. Position is the 2-rail telescope from the spec (show → season). Paused shows sit in grey with no nudges: grey because paused has no colour meaning.

6 · Run detail, with the telescope

The run as an object, not a table

A full-bleed band in the run's own tone with its key art, then the telescope exactly as specced (horizontal, 14 px minimum cell, read state as segments), then the spine. Every issue row has its cover. The check ring is the state glyph: filled ✓ for read, a blue ring for next, a dashed grey ring for read elsewhere.

7 · Completion: finishing a run

Finished, and what it opened

Shown on the run page after the last mark. There's no confetti. On arrival the run rail fills left to right once, over 900 ms (instantly under reduced motion). The reward is the record: how long it took, where it leaves the block, and what is now unlocked. The status badge stays grey “✓ read”, as specced, and green is used for the fills.

10 · Gaps filled · 5 Oct

Caught up, a block done, and the tablet

Three states that weren't drawn before. They follow on from section 7: you've just finished Uncanny X-Men. Each uses the accepted departures and the same tokens. The two new decisions they need are D13 and D14 in section 9.

10a · /comics, all caught up

Nothing is in progress, and both monthly runs are read to the latest issue. The Now slot says so plainly and lists what's coming out. Release dates are grey, because a date has no colour meaning. It offers the top of Up next once. Position uses the telescope's empty variant (A2.2): the track rail only, with no head and no brackets. The variant's own Start button is dropped, since the thumb bar already carries that verb (D13).

10b · Finishing a block

When the run you just finished is also the last unread run in its block, the completion screen steps up a level (D14). The art band fans out all the block's covers. The record moves from the run rail to the track rail: cell 1 fills once over --dur-fill, then the head ring moves to block 2 over --dur-4. Both happen instantly under reduced motion. The badge stays grey “✓ read”, and there is still no confetti.

10c · Tablet, 768–1023px (D-33)

Drawn at 768 × 1024, the narrowest case. There's a 72px navigation rail with each icon over an 11px label and no tooltips. The content sits in one column, with the desktop side rail (Ready, This year) stacked below it as two columns. The thumb bar stays at the bottom. The hero turns horizontal around a 150px cover, and the lens moves up beside it. The telescope keeps its phone sizing (--tele-* only changes at 900px) and fills the wider card, here W = 548. Interaction follows the pointer, so there are no hover reveals.
