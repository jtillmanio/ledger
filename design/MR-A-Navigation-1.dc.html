Mediums-Review · Part A

Navigation for five media

What it solves. The phone bar reads Ledger · Comics · Books · Watch and a gear, and it is full at 360px. Music and Games make five. A bar that grows with each medium has nowhere to grow, and the feel pass's desktop switch (a three-way segmented well) has the same limit at 192px. This part draws the options, picks one, and says where / and the pages that span every medium (Inbox, Progress, Activity) live.

The rule it lands on. Where all five fit with their names (the tablet rail, the 1280px sidebar), all five are always one tap away. Where they don't (the phone bar, the 1024px icon rail), the current medium is a button that switches it. The medium's own screens stay one tap away everywhere, as they are today.

A.1 · Options at 360px

Five media in the phone bar: what was tried

Each frame is the top of /books at 360 × 640, the narrowest width the app supports. A1 is today, for reference. A2–A4 were considered and rejected; A5 is the recommendation (MR-Q1).

  • A5, the switcher. The bar is Ledger, the current medium as a 44px button with a chevron (Books ▾, underlined in that medium’s accent as the link is today), then Search and Settings, each 44 × 44. At 360px that is 16 + 62 + 8 + 86 + 44 + 44 + 4 = 264px, leaving 96px spare whatever the medium’s name.
  • The cost is a second tap to change medium. Switching medium is rarer than moving inside one, and / already returns you to the medium you used last, so the common path stays one tap.
  • The tab row under the bar is unchanged in behaviour: the medium’s screens, then the pages that span media. Search returns to the bar because the switcher frees the width (part F, F-search).
A.2 · The switcher

Switch medium, and the tab row’s end

Tapping the medium opens a bottom sheet listing all five, each with what is in hand right now. The tab row scrolls sideways; its end holds the three pages that span media, after a divider.

  • The sheet is the shared Sheet (R1-11): labelled by its heading, focus on the heading, Tab trapped, Esc and Back close it and focus returns to the medium button. Rows are links; the current one is aria-current with a grey check, never a colour.
  • Each row’s second line is the medium’s Now in a few words, so the sheet doubles as a glance across everything in hand. It is read from the same views the dashboards use; nothing is fetched to draw it.
  • Tab row order: the medium’s items, a 1px --rule-2 divider (aria-hidden), then Inbox (with its mono count), Progress, Activity. The row keeps the current item scrolled into view (task 2.2i’s test holds it).
